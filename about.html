ABOUT VIGRID

Powering Rural India.
Empowering Lives.

VIGRID is on a mission to bring clean, reliable and affordable solar energy to every rural home in India. We design innovative 7-in-1 solar home kits that provide light, power and comfort – where it’s needed the most.

OUR STORY

From a Village Dream to a National Mission

VIGRID was founded with a simple but powerful dream – to solve the power problems in villages with smart, sustainable and affordable solutions.

Our founder, Vivek Kumar, grew up seeing the challenges rural families face – no electricity, no light for studies, limited opportunities.

With 8+ years of experience in electronics and a passion for innovation, he built VIGRID – a brand that delivers complete solar energy solutions in one box.

WHY WE EXIST

Because Every Home Deserves Light

Millions of rural homes still live without reliable electricity.

Children study in the dark, families struggle with daily tasks.

We believe clean energy should be a right, not a privilege.

VIGRID is here to bridge that gap – one home at a time.

OUR VALUES

Sustainability

We promote clean, green and sustainable energy for a better tomorrow.

Integrity

We are honest, transparent and committed to our customers and communities.

Innovation

We continuously innovate to create smart and reliable solar solutions.

Empathy

We care about people and work for their betterment with passion.

BUILT IN INDIA. MADE FOR INDIA.

Every VIGRID product is designed, developed and assembled in India to suit the real needs of rural families.
